## How each relationship works

### Fractional CMOs & marketing strategists

*Upstream referral sources · They refer to you · recurring · High (77)*

**Opportunity.** Fractional CMOs set strategy and budgets but need execution partners for SEO, content, and paid media.

**Why it makes commercial sense.** They control budgets across several clients and keep reusing reliable execution partners.

**Who decides.** Fractional CMO

**Trigger events**

- **A fractional CMO completes a marketing plan.** Execution needs a specialist. *Who notices:* Fractional CMO. *Refer when:* At plan approval. *Their gain:* Their strategy gets delivered.

**Mutual value.** They get dependable execution; you get recurring retainers.

**First step.** Offer transparent reporting that fractional CMOs can present to their own clients.

### Web developers & software agencies

*Complementary providers · Two-way referrals · recurring · High (77)*

**Opportunity.** Developers build sites and apps that need traffic and conversion; their clients ask who can market them.

**Why it makes commercial sense.** Launch is a natural handoff, and developers often lack marketing capability.

**Who decides.** Agency owner or project lead

**Trigger events**

- **A website or app launches.** The client needs traffic and conversion. *Who notices:* Developer. *Refer when:* Before launch. *Their gain:* Their launch succeeds.

**Mutual value.** Developers deliver successful launches; you can refer development work back.

**First step.** Agree on non-solicitation and a launch checklist that includes SEO and analytics handoff.

### Trademark lawyers (forced rebrands & domain changes)

*Upstream referral sources · They refer to you · occasional · High (73) · often overlooked*

**Opportunity.** When a trademark dispute or clearance problem forces a business to rename, it must move its website to a new domain. Done poorly, that can cost much of its search visibility.

**Why it makes commercial sense.** This is an urgent, budgeted moment where SEO migration expertise is valuable and the lawyer is the first adviser involved.

**Who decides.** Trademark lawyer or agent

**Trigger events**

- **A client receives a cease-and-desist or fails trademark clearance.** A rebrand and domain migration are needed. *Who notices:* Trademark lawyer. *Refer when:* When the rebrand decision is made. *Their gain:* Their client's business survives the rename intact.

**Mutual value.** Lawyers give clients a practical next step; you get urgent, well-funded migration projects. You can refer clients needing trademark clearance before a launch.

**First step.** Publish a rebrand-migration checklist and share it with IP lawyers. Offer a free migration-risk review for their clients.

### Recruiters filling marketing roles

*Upstream referral sources · Two-way referrals · occasional · Medium (69) · often overlooked*

**Opportunity.** Companies that struggle to hire or keep a marketing manager often need an agency to cover the gap, or permanently.

**Why it makes commercial sense.** Recruiters see failed searches and vacancies first, and agencies can refer placements when clients want to bring work in-house.

**Who decides.** Recruiter specialising in marketing and creative roles

**Trigger events**

**Mutual value.** Recruiters keep clients happy during vacancies; you refer in-house hiring needs to them.

**First step.** Propose a reciprocal arrangement with a marketing-focused recruiter.

### IT providers & web hosts during platform migrations

*Upstream referral sources · Two-way referrals · occasional · Medium (67) · often overlooked*

**Why it makes commercial sense.** Migrations are where organic traffic is most often lost, and IT providers know they are coming months in advance.

**Mutual value.** IT providers avoid post-launch complaints; you get project work. You can refer IT work back.

**First step.** Offer IT providers a fixed-price migration-protection package.

## Guardrails

- **Links are a by-product of genuine collaboration.** Shared-audience partnerships can produce earned, editorial links, but exchanging links for payment or as a condition of partnership violates search engine spam policies. Focus on resources and content that are useful to both audiences.
- **Partnerships are not paid-referral arrangements.** This analysis recommends building relationships based on mutual value. It does not assume that referral fees, commissions, exclusivity, or endorsements are permitted in your industry. Check professional, regulatory, and contractual rules before compensating anyone for referrals.
- **Outreach in Canada: CASL applies.** Commercial electronic messages generally need express or implied consent, sender identification, and a working unsubscribe mechanism. Implied consent can exist when a business email address is conspicuously published, the message relates to the recipient's role, and they have not said they don't want such messages. Keep records of how consent was obtained. Personal information you collect is also subject to privacy law (e.g. PIPEDA).
- **No automated outreach.** This tool does not contact anyone on your behalf. Every introduction should be personal, relevant to the recipient, and easy to decline.
